For artwork, review silhouette, joints, hand/prop contacts, foot contacts, acting, arcs, perspective, lighting and transitions. For audio, review likeness, intelligibility, pronunciation, naturalness and delivery. Preserve failed automated results and human ratings independently. No pretrained weights were retrained, no sentience/neural plasticity was established and no proprietary studio assets were inferred as available.
